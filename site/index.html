What stays on your computer

Your shoots, product photos, finished photos and own models are kept on your computer. Your OpenAI key lives in your system keychain.

  • OpenAI gets your product photos, plus the model, pose and scene for each photo, so it can make the photo.
  • Aaina gets nothing from you. The app only downloads the public catalogue of models, poses and scenes.
  • The app has no account and no tracking, no ads. The code is open for anyone to read.

Why it's free

Good product photos sell, but a photoshoot is out of reach for most people starting out. We used AI to rebuild a part of Aaina as a free, open-source app, so anyone starting a business, even without money for photoshoots or subscriptions, can have studio-quality photos from day one.

There is nothing to buy from us. You pay OpenAI only for the photos you make, a few cents each.

Design mockups

Aaina Desktop

A free, open-source app for Mac, Windows and Linux that makes model photos of your products. Everything is made and kept on your computer. These screens are static mockups of every step.

Kept on the computerShoots, photos, your own models, poses and scenes, and settings. Your OpenAI key lives in the system keychain.
Fetched from AainaOnly the studio catalogue: models, poses and scenes. It is cached, so browsing works offline.
Use the switcher at the bottom right of any screen to change between Mac, Windows and Linux, and between Light and Dark.

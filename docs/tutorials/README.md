# Aaina Desktop tutorials

Short guides to everything in the app, in the order you will meet it. Each one takes a few minutes to read. The pictures are from the app itself; the photos in them were made with Aaina Desktop.

1. [Getting started](01-getting-started.md): install, connect OpenAI, why there is a ChatGPT sign-in, and how to get an OpenAI key.
2. [Your first shoot](02-first-shoot.md): add a product, pick a model, poses and a scene, and make photos.
3. [Edit and save photos](03-edit-and-save.md): Make again, Fix something, Other colours, Frame and expression, and saving for your shop.
4. [Ghost mannequin and flat-lay](04-ghost-and-flat-lay.md): the product on its own, without a model.
5. [Your own models](05-own-models.md): a model from a description, or from photos of a real adult who agrees.
6. [Your own poses and scenes](06-own-poses-and-scenes.md): describe a pose or a place, or show one in a photo; plain colour scenes.
7. [Shoots and Library](07-shoots-and-library.md): find, reuse and tidy what you made.
8. [Settings, backup and privacy](08-settings.md): quality, folders, the offline catalogue, backups, and what is sent where.

There is also a [demo video](https://desktop.aaina.world/tutorials#video) that shows all of this in a few minutes.

## Costs at a glance

Aaina Desktop is free. The pictures are made by OpenAI with your own key, and OpenAI bills you directly. The app shows the rough cost on every button before anything is sent.

| What | About |
|---|---|
| A photo, standard quality | $0.05 |
| A photo, high quality | $0.20 |
| Fix something, Other colours (each colour) | $0.05 |
| A ghost mannequin angle or a flat-lay style | $0.05 |
| Your own pose or scene | $0.04 |
| Your own model: 3 faces, then the reference sheet | $0.15 + $0.20 |
| Reading a product | free with ChatGPT sign-in, otherwise a fraction of a cent |

If OpenAI refuses a photo, you are not charged for it.

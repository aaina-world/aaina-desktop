# Catalogue terms

Aaina Desktop downloads Aaina's public catalogue of studio models, poses and scenes. These terms are about those pictures. The app's code is separate and MIT licensed.

**You may**

- Use the catalogue inside Aaina Desktop to make photos.
- Use the photos you make for your business, including selling and advertising, without paying Aaina or crediting it.

**You may not**

- Copy, redistribute, resell or republish the catalogue pictures themselves (the models, reference sheets, poses and scenes).
- Scrape or bulk download the catalogue outside the app.
- Use the catalogue pictures to train or fine-tune any AI model.

**Changes.** Aaina may add, change or withdraw catalogue items at any time. Photos you have already made stay yours.

**No promises.** The catalogue is provided as it is, without warranty.

Questions: open a discussion on the project's GitHub page.

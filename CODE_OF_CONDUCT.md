# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient with newcomers, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. We do not tolerate harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the project is represented.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close threads or block people who break these rules.

**Reporting.** Email CONDUCT-CONTACT-PLACEHOLDER (to be set by the maintainers). Reports are handled privately.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban.

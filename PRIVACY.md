# Privacy

Aaina Desktop is built so that Aaina never sees your work.

**On your computer.** Shoots, product photos, finished photos, own models, own poses and scenes, and settings are stored on your computer, in the app's data folder and your photos folder. Aaina has no copy.

**Your OpenAI key.** It is kept in your system keychain, never in files or logs, and is sent only to OpenAI.

**What is sent to OpenAI.** To make a photo, the app sends OpenAI your product photos plus the model, pose and scene for that photo (and any words you typed). OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy apply to what you send. OpenAI bills your account.

**What the app asks Aaina for.** Only downloads of the public catalogue (studio models, poses and scenes) from static.aaina.world. Nothing about you or your work is uploaded. As with any web request, the server sees your IP address when you download.

**Updates.** The app may ask GitHub whether a newer version exists.

**No tracking in the app.** No analytics, no crash reports, no accounts, no ads.

**Deleting.** Delete a shoot in the app to remove it, or remove the app's data folder and your photos folder to remove everything.

## This website

desktop.aaina.world counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not identify you. The app itself sends nothing to Aaina.
